Privacy

Draft for a hackathon project. Have a lawyer review it before any public launch.

Without an account

We do not save your bill, PIN or location. Your PIN, bill amount and state are sent to our server to calculate results.

With Google sign-in

We store your name, email and a Google identifier, plus each check you run while signed in (state, units, score and bill amount). We use this only to show your trends and tips. We do not store your Consumer ID, address or exact location.

Location

If you tap "Use my location" your browser shares coordinates with an address lookup service (OpenStreetMap Nominatim) to find your PIN and state. You can decline and pick your state instead.

Shared rates

If you choose to share tariff rates they are stored without your name, for review.

Third parties

Weather data comes from Open-Meteo, and state lookup from India Post's directory. The helper chat may use an AI service; do not type personal details into it.

Your choices

Delete your account and history from the menu at any time. Messages sent through the Contact page are kept so we can reply.
