About WattBack

WattBack turns the one number every household already sees, the monthly electricity bill, into a clear picture: how many units you used, how much of that was cooling, whether you crossed a costly slab, and how you compare with homes of your size in similar weather.

It costs nothing, needs no smart meter and never controls your appliances. Rewards go to households that use less than expected for their size, so big families are not ranked behind small ones.

WattBack was built by a two-person team (team meteor) for HackCult '26 at IIT Gandhinagar, on the GEDA Mission LiFE citizen engagement problem statement. It is a hackathon project, not an official GEDA product.

How accurate is it?

Where we hold your state's tariff, units are close to exact. Where we don't, we show a range and label it an estimate. You can always enter the slabs printed on your own bill.
